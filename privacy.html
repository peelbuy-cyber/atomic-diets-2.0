Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Atomic Diets (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our subscription health and fitness platform.

1) Information we collect

When you use Atomic Diets, you may provide information such as your name, age, gender, height, weight, activity level, country, diet type, and workout timing. We may also collect account details from Firebase Authentication (e.g., email) and payment status from Stripe.

2) How we use information

  • To generate your personalized diet and workout plan.
  • To provide subscription features and unlock Pro content.
  • To store your reports and progress in your account dashboard.
  • To improve our product experience and troubleshoot issues.

3) AI-generated content

Your inputs may be used to generate personalized recommendations using AI systems. This is for educational and planning purposes and not medical advice.

4) Payment information

Stripe processes payments. We do not directly store your full payment card information.

5) Data storage and third parties

We use third-party services such as Firebase (authentication and database) and Stripe (billing). These providers may store and process your data according to their own policies.

6) Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You can contact us to request assistance.

7) Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: support@atomic-diets.example
